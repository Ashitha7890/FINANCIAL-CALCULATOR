Limits worth stating. Installments are level and the rate constant — grace periods, balloons and step-up schedules are not modelled. The annual rate is divided by the number of cycles, which is the nominal convention: 16% over 2 cycles costs 16.6% effective. Benefits are forecasts; the 25% haircut exists because of that, but it does not make a badly estimated benefit line safe. This sizes affordability only — character, collateral and the coop's exposure limits sit outside it.
